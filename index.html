Ygg-Rebirth

The world is still being built.

We are sharing the journey while keeping plenty left to discover in-game. What you see today is only a glimpse of the world taking root.

Around four centuries ago, civilization began to retreat. Roads were abandoned, territories were lost, and much of the world slowly returned to wilderness.

Now, something is changing. You awaken near Sèvegarde, carrying fragmented memories and a mysterious Mark of Yggdrasil.

Your past is uncertain. Your future path is yours to discover.
